Frequently asked questions

What's included in the $300 price?

A complete 1-3 page website designed around your business, built to look great on phones and computers, plus one round of revisions before launch. Additional pages beyond the first 3 are $100 each.

Do I need to provide anything?

Just tell us about your business, your hours/location, and share any logo, photos, or branding you already have. Don't have any of that? We can help with placeholder content to get you started.

How long does it take?

Most simple sites are ready for review within a few business days after we have your information.

Do I have to pay for hosting?

Hosting isn't required to be purchased through us, but we offer an optional low-cost monthly plan if you'd like us to handle hosting and small updates for you.

Can I ask for changes later?

Yes. One round of revisions is included before launch, and additional updates can be requested afterward for a small fee.

Do you only build websites for local New Jersey businesses?

No. While we're based in New Jersey, we build websites for small business owners anywhere in the United States. Everything is handled remotely by email, so your location doesn't matter.
